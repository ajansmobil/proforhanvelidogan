Quality policy: In cardiac and vascular surgery services, centering patient safety, evidence-based practice, and adopting a culture of continuous improvement.

Clinical processes

Diagnosis–treatment in line with current guidelines; standard protocols for coronary, valve, TAVI, aortic and vascular interventions.

Safety

Identification, surgical site marking, safe surgical checklists, antibiotic prophylaxis, and infection monitoring.

Monitoring

Monitoring of complications and quality indicators; patient and family feedback; event reporting and learning.

Inter-team training, regular record keeping, and a governance mindset derived from specialized hospital and university experience support our quality approach. The policy text is periodically reviewed; updates are published on the website.
