Assoc. Prof. Dr. Orhan Veli Doğan

Cardiovascular surgeon with extensive clinical and academic experience. Graduate of Hacettepe University Faculty of Medicine; residency and associate professorship at Ankara Dışkapı Training and Research Hospital; professorship at Hatay Mustafa Kemal University. Former director of cardiovascular surgery at Private Defne and Private Akademi hospitals.
