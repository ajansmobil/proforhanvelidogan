Our Vision: A reference recognized by patients and peers along the Gaziantep, Hatay and Cyprus line; providing reliable, evidence-based and accessible services in cardiovascular surgery.

Clinical

Current treatment options in coronary, valve, aorta, congenital heart, varicose veins and peripheral vessel diseases.

Technical

Minimally invasive approaches, TAVI, EVAR/TEVAR, endovascular and open surgery integrity.

Academic

Scientific productivity with thesis supervision, peer review, invited talks and session chairmanships.

Academic roots are supported by Hacettepe University, Ankara Dışkapı Training and Research Hospital, Mustafa Kemal University, Trabzon Ahi Evren, Sakarya University and international (e.g., Osh-Kardio) experience. Turkish Thoracic, Cardiovascular and Vascular Surgery Association and Turkish Vascular Surgery Association memberships strengthen our commitment to professional standards.

Patient safety, transparency and continuous learning are integral parts of our vision; we aim to keep treatment quality up to date by following innovations.
