Conference peer review, maintains the quality of the scientific program by evaluating the scientific level, originality, and ethical appropriateness of submitted abstracts.

Prof. Dr. Orhan Veli Doğan, has performed peer review duties for abstracts under the 11. Turkish Cardiovascular Surgery Congress and 12. Turkish Cardiovascular Surgery Congress. During this process, topics such as methodology, statistical appropriateness, and clinical originality value are reviewed in detail.

Role of the reviewer

  • Alignment with conference themes
  • Abstract and full text evaluation
  • Constructive feedback and suggestions
