Mission: To provide patient-centered, transparent, and evidence-based treatment in cardiovascular surgery; ensure reliable access with clinical experience in Gaziantep, Hatay and Cyprus.

As Assoc. Prof. Dr. Orhan Veli Doğan and his team, our mission is to provide patient-centered, evidence-based, and reliable healthcare services in the field of cardiovascular surgery.

We apply current methods in the diagnostic and treatment processes of our patients according to national and international guidelines. With our experienced staff in coronary bypass, valve surgery, TAVI, vascular and endovascular interventions, we aim to be a reference center in our region.

We value education and scientific development; thesis supervision, peer review in journals and congresses, and our participation in national and international scientific meetings are part of this mission. Patient safety and quality standards are always our priority.

The clinical experience gained during the periods of being Director of Cardiovascular Surgery at Özel Defne Hospital and Özel Akademi Hospital, combined with the history of Mustafa Kemal University Faculty of Medicine and Ankara Dışkapı Training Hospital, supports service quality.

While realizing our mission, we seek transparent communication with patients and their relatives, adhere to ethical principles, and adopt a continuous improvement mindset. In the field of cardiovascular surgery, we prioritize evidence-based practices in adult cardiac surgery, congenital heart surgeries, and vascular and endovascular interventions. Our membership in the Turkish Thoracic, Cardiovascular and Vascular Surgery Association and the Turkish Vascular Surgery Association maintains our commitment to professional standards. Our goal is to offer the most suitable treatment option for every patient and maintain high patient satisfaction. This mission is fueled by education and clinical experience from Hacettepe University Faculty of Medicine, Ankara Dışkapı Training Hospital, and Mustafa Kemal University Faculty of Medicine. You can reach us via our contact page for appointment and information requests. Our team, experienced in cardiovascular surgery, varicose vein treatment, coronary bypass, and valve surgery, is at your service. We place great emphasis on continuous education and scientific development to realize our mission.
